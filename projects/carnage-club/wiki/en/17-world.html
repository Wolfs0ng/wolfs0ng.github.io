The world & atmosphere

A fallen hunters’ order, gothic spaces, and a power that leaves its mark.

A place with a past#

Carnage Club grew from a fallen hunters’ order. Its architecture, objects, and encounters retain traces of an earlier life. The hunter comes to know the place through the journey.

A gothic visual language#

Dark spaces, old ritual motifs, restrained gold, and blood-red accents establish the tone. Illustrated scenes and characters support a dangerous world with rules of its own.

The mechanics belong to the world#

Weapons, blood abilities, and combat experience connect to the identity of a hunter. Character development becomes part of the journey rather than an isolated set of numbers.

The causes of events, hidden connections, and the characters’ later fates remain discoveries for the game.
