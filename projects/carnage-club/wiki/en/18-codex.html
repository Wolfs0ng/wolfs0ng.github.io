Understand a new tool#

Hints and the Codex explain combat resources, actions, and effects. They provide context for what you see in the interface: what a stat means, the role of a defense, and why an effect matters.

Explanation and observation#

Reference material explains a mechanic; the battle log shows its part in a particular exchange. Together they connect the rules to your own experience of playing.

Where to begin#

Start with the game overview. For duels, read about combat and resources. Character development has its own pages on builds and equipment.

This guide explains the character of the project while leaving narrative discoveries for your own playthrough.
