Health, Guard & Stamina

Three resources create different kinds of pressure and shape the options available next round.

1 min read

On this page
  • Health
  • Guard
  • Stamina
  • Read them together

Health#

Health determines whether a participant remains in the duel. Uncovered attacks and certain effects threaten it directly. Other mechanics let a hunter recover Health within the options provided by the build.

Guard#

Guard supports blocking and the ability to withstand pressure. Wearing down a defensive stance changes the situation even while Health still looks comfortable. A broken Guard makes the exchange more dangerous.

Stamina#

Stamina powers parrying and many active abilities. Spending it connects a strong action now with the options available in the following round.

Read them together#

The three bars describe different aspects of the hunter’s condition. High Health does not replace depleted Guard or Stamina. Equipment, progression, and active effects shape how a build handles each resource.
