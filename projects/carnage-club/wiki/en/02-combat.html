Tactical duels

How a duel works

Combat unfolds in rounds: both participants attack, defend, and see the consequences of their choices.

1 min read

On this page
  • Decisions before action
  • What shapes the outcome
  • What carries into the next round

Decisions before action#

Each round, you choose attack and defense directions. You also select a defense type and, when appropriate, an active ability. The opponent makes its own choices; resolution brings these decisions together.

The turn-based format gives you time to think. Consider the hunter’s condition, the options provided by equipment, and the behavior you have already observed.

What shapes the outcome#

An attack direction meets the corresponding defense. An uncovered zone, a block, and a parry produce different consequences for health, defensive guard, and stamina. Abilities, perks, and status effects can add to the exchange.

Consider the whole round: landing your own attack does not explain what happened in return.

What carries into the next round#

Changed resources and active effects influence the next decision. The battle log explains the actions, while the adaptive opponent draws on its accumulated experience.

Continue with directions, Block and Parry, and combat resources.
