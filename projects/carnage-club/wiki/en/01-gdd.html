Introduction

What is Carnage Club?

A mobile turn-based RPG about tactical duels, finding your own fighting style, and discovering a dark world at your own pace.

2 min read

The core idea#

Carnage Club combines one-on-one duels, character development, and exploration of a fallen hunters’ order. At its heart is an exchange of decisions: you observe the opponent while it gains experience from your actions.

The world remembers your habits. Familiar tactics can remain useful, but repetition makes you more predictable. Your build, resource management, and attention to the previous round work together.

The game loop#

  1. Explore. Move across the map, find encounters, and discover available routes.
  2. Prepare. Combine equipment, attributes, abilities, and perks.
  3. Decide. Choose attack and defense directions and keep track of your resources.
  4. Reflect. The battle log explains the outcome and helps you reconsider the next turn.

Three pillars#

  • Tactical duels: the choices of both participants shape the result.
  • Your own build: different tools support different ways of playing.
  • Atmosphere and discovery: encounters, items, and dialogue gradually reveal the world.

About this guide#

This is a public introduction to a game in development. It explains the main mechanics while leaving the story’s discoveries in the game. Screens show the current version; details may change during development.
