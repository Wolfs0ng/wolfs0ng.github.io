A lasting part of the build#

Perks complement a hunter without requiring a separate action every round. Some change combat capabilities; others react to events such as a successful attack or parry.

Conditions matter#

A reactive perk becomes relevant when its triggering event occurs. Its usefulness depends on the hunter’s style, equipment, and decisions. A perk supporting a particular weapon or defense should be considered in that context.

Interactions give a build character#

Perks can help a hunter withstand pressure, develop bleeding effects, or support parrying. The progression screen shows examples, while the battle log helps reveal their effects in an exchange.
