Opponents that learn

Experience from previous exchanges influences an opponent’s later decisions.

1 min read

The duel develops#

An opponent takes completed actions and their consequences into account. Repeated directions, familiar moves, and interaction outcomes gradually form experience.

The present situation matters too: resources and available options change the value of decisions. Observe the opponent in the context of the current fight.

Campaign memory#

Knowledge about the player’s habits persists between sessions within a campaign. The end of an individual duel does not erase all experience of those interactions.

The player learns too#

Adaptation creates a mutual process. The enemy gains experience from your actions; the battle scene and log let you see the consequences of its choices. A changed habit or a different build gives the exchange a new direction.
